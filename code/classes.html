Aqidah

Introductory 'Aqidah

Explore the core tenets of Ahlus Sunnah wal Jama'ah. This course covers the essential manners of a learner alongside a clear breakdown of correct and incorrect beliefs regarding Allah, the Prophets, and the Unseen. Students will also discover the vital roles of the Pious Predecessors (as-Salaf) and the chain of narration (al-Isnad) in understanding and preserving the faith.
